# RouteViz

See where the cars in Mini Motorways are going.

- **Click a car** to show its route in the car's colour: the road ahead, plus the part already driven, grayed out. Click again to hide it.
- **Click a house or destination** to show every car driving to or from it. Click again to hide them.
- **Click a destination's pin** to show the car coming to fulfil it.
- **C** clears all routes. **Alt+C** hides or shows them all.
- Where routes share a lane, that stretch pulses through each car's colour.

The mod is read-only: it never changes the simulation, so it can't affect your score.

## Settings

After the first start, edit `BepInEx\config\motorways.routeviz.cfg` (in r2modman: *Config editor*): route width, arrow speed, gray amount, click radius, keys and sounds.

## Requirements

Windows, Steam version of Mini Motorways. Depends on `BepInExPack_MiniMotorways`, which sets up BepInEx for this game (the mod manager installs it automatically).
